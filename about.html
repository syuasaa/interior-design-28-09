Studio Story

Building a language of material honesty, clean geometries, and raw emotional power.

Founded in Yerevan, FORMA is an architectural workspace driven by a deep reverence for raw materials and spatial purity. We approach interior architecture not as decoration, but as the quiet configuration of light, mass, and human flow.

Every residence and commercial space we program is built upon close structural observation. We work intimately with master artisans, stonemasons, and millworkers to ensure our layouts manifest with absolute technical rigor and lasting material permanence.
